# SugarLite - Smart Blood Sugar Management App

> SugarLite is a professional blood sugar monitoring, trend analysis and health management application. Record, analyze, and discover what foods work best for your body.

## AI-Friendly Pages

- [Complete site information (distilled)](https://sugarlite.top/llms-full.html): structured HTML version of this site, ideal for LLM consumption

## Core Features

- Blood sugar trend tracking: log pre- and post-meal glucose values and view automatic trend curves
- Food logging: quickly record daily meals with a rich food database and custom entries
- Glucose index: pre/post-meal pairing analysis identifies foods that are friendly or risky for your glucose
- Exercise logging: record activity and see how different intensities affect glucose fluctuations
- AI insights: personalized recommendations generated from your records
- Cloud sync: data syncs automatically after login for a seamless cross-device experience
